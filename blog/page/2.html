What is privileged access management (PAM)? A plain-English guide for infrastructure teams

September 2, 2026 · 4 min read
Orion Belt
Maintainers

Privileged access management (PAM) is the set of controls that decides who may use powerful accounts and systems, when, with whose approval, and keeps a record of what they did. "Privileged" means access that can change production, read sensitive data or turn off other controls: root on a server, a database admin login, a cloud admin role.
